About s106calculator.co.uk

Reviewed by Chartered Planner (MRTPI) and Chartered Surveyor (MRICS) · 2026-06-21
Direct answer · 49 words
s106calculator.co.uk is published by Digital Signet. Contribution rates are reviewed by a chartered planner (MRTPI) and a chartered surveyor (MRICS). The supported-LPA tier is intentionally thin (10 authorities) because per-LPA rates have to be hand-verified from each council's adopted SPD; the alternative is fabrication.

Editorial review

Every contribution rate, every head-of-contribution mechanic, and every statutory reference on this site has been reviewed by:

  • A chartered planner (MRTPI) for the planning-policy and SPD references.
  • A chartered surveyor (MRICS) for the viability and residual land value mechanics.

The current review date is shown on every page as a small section stamp under the H1. The supporting registry of sources is at /sources.

Refresh cadence

BCIS All-in TPI rebases land quarterly; the site rebases on the first business day of each new quarter. SPDs are re-checked annually and on any LPA Local Plan refresh. The statutory layer is monitored weekly via the Planning Inspectorate decision feed and the legislation.gov.uk RSS for Town and Country Planning instruments.

Scope and limits

  • The estimator covers the six standard heads of contribution. Other deed clauses (notably bespoke open-space typology splits, BNG management plans, off-site highways works) are not priced.
  • Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland obligations are out of scope; the regime here is England only.
  • Strategic infrastructure (SCSI, joint-authority works, NSIP development consent orders) is out of scope and routed via the Infrastructure Levy pilots where applicable.

Why the supported-LPA tier is thin

England has 333 LPAs. Building a 333-row table requires hand-verifying each council's adopted SPD, the BCIS rebase, and the CIL charging schedule. We will not fabricate rates to chase the long-tail. The starting cohort is 10 hand-verified authorities; the next cohort is prioritised by request volume in the contact log.

Contact

Editorial corrections, missing-LPA requests, and SPD links: contact the Digital Signet editorial team via the contact form linked in the footer.
