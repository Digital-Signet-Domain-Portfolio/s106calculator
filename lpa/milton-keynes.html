Milton Keynes City Council: section 106 contributions

Reviewed by Chartered Planner (MRTPI) and Chartered Surveyor (MRICS) · 2026-06-21
Direct answer · 50 words
Milton Keynes City Council carries an affordable housing target of 31% under the adopted SPD (2019). The per-dwelling shortfall commuted sum is approximately £42,000. Education, open space, healthcare, transport and monitoring fee rates are listed below, each BCIS-rebased to 2026 Q2. No CIL; MK Tariff persists in the urban-expansion areas alongside S106.
