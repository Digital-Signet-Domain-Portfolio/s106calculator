Direct answer · 47 words
A section 106 calculator estimates the planning-obligation £ payable under s.106 of the Town and Country Planning Act 1990. For a typical 50-unit urban-brownfield scheme in a non-London LPA, expect £12,000 to £18,000 per dwelling across the six standard heads of contribution, before any CIL or BNG layering.
S106 §1:100 · methodology

How the estimate is built

The 10 supported LPAs (Birmingham, Manchester, Leeds, Bristol, Cornwall, RBKC, Camden, Cambridge, Milton Keynes, plus the regional fallback) carry rates hand-extracted from each council's adopted SPD and rebased to BCIS All-in TPI 2026 Q2 [BCIS All-in TPI]. The 6 heads of contribution follow the structure of CIL Regs 2010 reg 122[CIL Regs 2010 reg 122] and the PPG Planning Obligations guidance [PPG Planning Obligations]. The monitoring fee is constrained by the CIL (Amendment) Regs 2019 reg 4[CIL (Amendment) Regs 2019], which formalised the £500 to £1,200 per-obligation range. The viability red/amber/green chip uses the £14,500/dwelling median across the 10 supported LPAs (urban-brownfield, 20% AH delivered, no CIL or BNG).

S106 §1:50 · recent changes

What changed in 2026

29 Apr 2026
English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026 received Royal Assent [English Devolution and Community Empowerment Act 2026]. It creates strategic authorities with strategic-planning (Spatial Development Strategy) powers but does not amend s.106; LPA obligation negotiation is unchanged. Detail in /regulation/devolution-act-2026.
13 Jul 2026
BNG amendments confirmed (SI 2026/790): a 0.2ha small-site exemption and removal of the self-build exemption, for applications on or after 6 Aug 2026. See /bng/exemptions-2026.
Dec 2024
NPPF refresh: viability para 58 retained with minor recalibration of policy-compliant tariff treatment [NPPF (Dec 2024)]. See /regulation/nppf-dec-2024.
